# CRM-Sales-Performance-Revenue-Analytics-Project
An end-to-end CRM dashboard analyzing lead conversion, sales pipelines, regional performance, and agent productivity. This repository showcases actionable sales intelligence built to optimize lead distribution, forecast deals, and accelerate closing cycles.

# 📈 Key Executive Insights:

### Overall Pipeline Value: 
$8.3M total deal value across 3,000 leads.  

### Conversion Efficiency: 
12% overall conversion rate with 348 closed deals generating $931.3K in revenue.

### Top Product Segment: 
SaaS leads the lead volume (43%) and conversion rate (35%). Services drive the highest overall deal value (40%).

### Top Geographic Market: 
The Netherlands leads in conversion rate at 15.0%, followed by Portugal (13.7%) and Spain (13.2%).

### Top Sales Performer: 
Laura Thompson generated $2.048M in total deal value, significantly outperforming sales targets.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### Focus Mid-Market Expansion: 
1. Medium-sized organizations (201–500 employees) drive over 50% of closed deal revenue ($541.9K). Increasing sales touchpoints here offers the highest immediate ROI.
2. Optimize SaaS Lead Nurturing: SaaS captures 43% of total leads and the highest conversion rate (35%). Scaling marketing efforts into this offering will boost overall deal volume.
3. Reallocate Sales Focus in DACH Region: Conversion rates in Germany (9.5%) and Switzerland (9.7%) lag behind Western Europe.
4. Tailored regional sales strategies or local onboarding support could bridge this gap.
